# Brag plan · ScamSuno V3

ScamSuno gives people a safe place to practise evaluating a fictional bank call. Its central interaction is **Trust or Trap**, an educational simulation where people answer in their own words and can verify a notice independently. The angle is **recognition is easy to confuse with trust**. Open on an unmistakable incoming call, reveal the real V3 locale controls, explain the supervised voice slot, and close with the practical habit of checking a known channel.

Use a polished editorial tone. Brand cues come from the V3 incoming-call panel, waveform mark, paper background (#fff9f0), dark plum (#292238), peach (#ffad94), red (#a83238) and green (#d9edb9). Use Segoe UI with Nirmala UI for regional labels. Format is 1920×1080, 30 fps, 60 seconds per the approved competition brief. The current edit contains the actual public app entry and an explicitly illustrated, silent voice slot for the future Android recording.
