# Demo and submission

The [Gnani challenge page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship) lists November 10, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. IST as the final deadline. The specification’s internal target is 4 p.m. IST that day.

## Rehearsal

1. Open ScamSuno, pick your language and start the KYC freeze call.
2. Continue without the microphone and answer. For a dry run, choose go along, ask who is calling, ask for a moment, then say you will call the bank yourself.
3. Open the debrief. Point out the artificial deadline, the code request and the caller’s attempt to discourage verification.
4. Download the PNG and open the shared page. The card contains outcome and training feedback.

## Record the live voice demo after verification

Use a deployed HTTPS URL on a phone. Confirm the caller audio was generated by Timbre and that the real Prisma response appears in the debrief. Speak a fictional reply at each beat.

- **0–5 s** Incoming call, simulation label and answer.
- **5–18 s** “हाँ, बोलिए।” then “आप कौन-सी शाखा से बोल रहे हैं?” Show the transcript confirmation at least once.
- **18–33 s** After the code request, say “एक मिनट दीजिए।” Then respond to the last push with “नहीं, मैं खुद बैंक के आधिकारिक ऐप में जाँच करूँगी।”
- **33–46 s** Independent verification reveal, red flags and one official-source quote.
- **46–55 s** Open the reply trace to show the actual Prisma model and request ID. Name the Timbre model from the manifest and the configured classifier.
- **55–60 s** Show the share card and public URL. Mark the recording as edited for length if there are cuts.

If a spoken reply is misheard, re-record or show the fallback clearly. Mention a benchmark only after an evaluation result records its conditions and denominator.

## Tamil and Telugu demo lines

A fixed script responds to a small set of reply intents, and the debrief links the caller’s tactics to official guidance. The demo shows the decision to end the call and verify through an independently obtained bank channel.
