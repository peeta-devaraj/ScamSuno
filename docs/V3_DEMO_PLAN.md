# V3 demo and human acceptance

Not yet recorded. Use a protected deployed HTTPS preview only after billing and bounded admission are verified. A fixture recording must be labelled; never splice synthetic transcripts into a purported live call.

## Reproducible phone checklist

Record date, deployment/commit, device model, OS/browser version, locale, network and microphone permission state. Use fictional speech only; do not save audio or actual secrets. Check Android Chrome and iOS Safari separately.

1. Open Trust or Trap, confirm educational disclosure and hidden category. Answer after a user gesture; hear the opening and read its matching caption.
2. Ask a natural question, confirm actual STT text/model provenance, then send. Record observed success/failure; do not infer accuracy from one reply. Repeat with a corrected transcript and confirm typed origin.
3. Challenge the claim or express uncertainty. Open the independent fictional panel; ensure its claim finding is separate from caller identity.
4. End during recording, model wait and playback in separate bounded runs. Verify microphone/playback stop and late results cannot reopen the call.
5. Check debrief, observed/untested distinctions, no penalty for cautious exit, and no private text in sharing. Repeat microphone denial with complete typed fallback.

## 60–90 second demonstration

Show an actual incoming call, one natural multilingual exchange, uncertainty, a trusted-channel check or safe exit, then the revealed scenario and evidence-based debrief. Explain that voice/claims alone do not authenticate a caller. State device/test conditions and limitations. Keep the operator able to pause traffic immediately.

## Small pilot protocol

Pending participant consent and actual participation. Use a balanced, counterbalanced sequence of the three fictional scenarios; record only anonymized action categories and voluntary usability feedback. Report actual sample size, completed sessions, blanket-refusal patterns, verification actions and comprehension issues. Treat this as exploratory usability evidence; do not infer learning improvement or fraud prevention without an appropriate study design. No pilot results exist yet.
