# ScamSuno brand guide

**ScamSuno** is the product. **Can you trust this call?** is the tagline. **Trust or Trap** is the flagship practice mode. The short description is “Multilingual AI-powered scam-awareness training through fictional voice calls.” Use English, Tamil, Telugu and Hindi copy in their own scripts. Use “Single-scenario practice” and “Scripted practice” for the separate modes. Keep generation numbers in engineering records.

## Visual direction

The reference is the original video's dark hook and closing frame, preserved in the local pre-brand archive. The unified experience uses deep plum, lifted violet cards, coral actions, warm cream headlines and soft lavender body text. A thin orbit around the incoming card references the video. The small text and decorative numbering use monospace where the locale supports it. The light video interludes use the same cream and plum pair.

Use the waveform path from [the app icon set](../../src/components/icons.tsx). On dark backgrounds, give the mark a coral rounded square, dark waveform and cream wordmark with a coral “Suno”. Avoid invented logos and generic security gradients. The [social art](../../public/brand/scamsuno-social.svg) puts the name, tagline and educational disclosure inside the center square of the 1200×630 card so the text survives landscape and square crops. The published image is its PNG. [Design tokens](DESIGN_TOKENS.md)

## Interaction and disclosure

- Show “Educational simulation” and make the fictional status clear before answering. Keep the private pause disclosure and working scripted link while admission is closed. Do not add an enabled-looking start control in that state.
- The call status, End action, transcript confirmation and independent check remain visible text and controls. Use named evidence states in the debrief and reveal the caller category only there.
- The demo cut can omit editor overlays. Keep “Android footage pending” until authentic footage replaces it. The synthetic English provider check is separate evidence. Label illustrations of the voice workflow, verification and debrief. Use `scamsuno.vercel.app` on the end card; link to [Classic](https://scamsuno-classic.vercel.app) when referring to scripted practice.
- Export brand artwork with its real product name and no deployment URL. Enable absolute social and canonical links only from an explicitly configured public Production origin. Review LinkedIn/X link previews once the approved origin serves the artwork.
